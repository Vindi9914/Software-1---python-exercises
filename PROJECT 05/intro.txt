Welcome to the Adventure Game!

You are a player in a village.

Your first task is to pick apples in the Village.
Every time you collect 100 apples, a key appear in the cave.
There are 5 keys in total.

After getting a key, you must escape through the Forest.
There are bears in the forest.

If you successfully escape from the bears, you can reach the Palace.
Use your key to open the Palace door and take the sword.
Then you become a Warrior.

As a Warrior, you must defeat enemies.
When you defeat an enemy, a diamond appears.
Collect 5 diamonds to become the winner!

Good luck!
