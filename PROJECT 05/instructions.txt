GAME INSTRUCTIONS

1. Start the game in the Village.
2. Pick apples in the Village.
3. Collect 100 apples to get one key and it will appear in the cave.
4. You can return to the Village and continue picking apples.
5. There are 5 keys in total.
6. Enter the cave and take a key.
7. Go through the Forest.
8. Escape from the bears.
9. If you successfully escape, go to the Palace.
10. Use the key to open the Palace door.
11. Take the sword and become a Warrior.
12. Defeat enemies using the sword.
13. A diamond appears after defeating an enemy.
14. Collect 5 diamonds to win the game.
