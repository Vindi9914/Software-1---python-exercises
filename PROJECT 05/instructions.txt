HOW TO PLAY

1. Enter your name and age. This game is intended for players aged 13 and over.
2. Start in the Village and collect apples. Each time you collect apples, you get 100 apples 
and one key becomes available, up to five keys.
3. Visit the Cave and take a key.
4. Choose one of two routes:
   * Forest Path: choose to escape quietly through the trees to avoid the bear and reach the Palace.
   * Meadow Path: choose to move away slowly to avoid the snake and reach the Palace.

5. At the Palace, use your key to open the door and collect the sword.
6. Become a Warrior and fight enemies to collect diamonds.
7. Collect five diamonds to win the adventure.
8. Use the Save and Exit option to save your progress and continue later.
