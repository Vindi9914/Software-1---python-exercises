WELCOME TO THE ADVENTURE: GUARDIANS OF THE GREEN WORLD!

A peaceful village is surrounded by a forest and a meadow. 
These natural habitats are home to many wild animals and plants.
Your adventure will take you through different paths before you reach the Palace.
You can choose the Forest Path, where you must escape from a bear, or the Meadow Path, 
where you must move away from a snake carefully. Your choices will help you continue your journey 
safely while respecting wildlife.
Your main objective is to reach the Palace, become a Warrior, and collect five diamonds by defeating enemies.
Explore carefully, make wise choices, and good luck!
